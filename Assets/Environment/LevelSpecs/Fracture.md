# Fracture

Built by `Assets/Editor/FractureBuilder.cs` (`Turbo Tag/Levels/Rebuild Fracture`, job `BuildFracture`). Greybox. ~130 × 130 m.

**Idea:** the broken-apart map. The mainland is split into five shards by rifts down to the kill plane, and there are pits
cut straight through the middle of the shards too. Danger is placed, not everywhere: every interior pit has a low
orange curb (0.5 m: you never walk in by accident, a hop gets you in), every rift bridge has curbs, and buildings,
tunnels and courtyards sit away from the edges. More height steps than Skyreach: 0 / 8 / 16 / 26 / 30 / 36.

## Shards
| Shard | Floor | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Centre isle | 0 | The Spire: base 10 (cross tunnels), middle 20, top 30 with an out-ledge, Spire lift from the isle. Bridges S, E, W; a 6 m jump N. |
| South | 0 | Pits: the Well (8 × 8), the Crack (20 × 4, a plank across the middle). South Hall, Pump House tunnel. South bluff (8): ramp + building climb, hut, bridge at 8 to the West shard. |
| East | 0 | Pits: the Sinkhole (12 × 12, a shuttle rides across), the Slot. East Bunker tunnel, shed. East terrace (8): ramp + climb house. East block (16): building climb. |
| West | 8 | Tunnel in from the centre bridge (at 0) to a sunken cave courtyard (0, open above, ramp up). A pit through the whole shard. Watch rock (16), hut, tunnel block. Long ramp bridge down to the North shard. |
| North | 0 | Twin pits. High mesa (16): tunnel + hall inside, ramp up its west face (arched over the tunnel), building climb. North tower (26): ramp across the mesa, climb. Crown (36): climb, Lookout hut. Sky lift (ground → Sky deck 36), Sky bridge (one 3 m hop) to the Crown. |

## Routes
- Easy loop on the ground: South ↔ Centre ↔ East ↔ North (bridges), West via the centre tunnel or the bluff bridge / ramp bridge.
- Risky shortcuts: Centre → North jump (6 m), South → East jump (7 m), the Crack plank, the Sinkhole shuttle, the Sky bridge hop.
- Up: ramps (22–28°) for every tier, building climbs as the hard way, lifts to the Spire top and the Sky deck.
