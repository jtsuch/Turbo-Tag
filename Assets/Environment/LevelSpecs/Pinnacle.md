# Pinnacle — level plan (draft for approval)

Greybox with Justin's kit. Built by a rebuildable builder script (`Turbo Tag/Levels/Rebuild Pinnacle`).
North is +Z. Grid 4 m. Kill plane (DeathZone) 40 m below the main floor; nothing under the platforms but the pit.

## Platforms
- **Main (110 x 110 m):** west half at floor 0, east half a raised plateau at +6 m. Joined by a ramp, stairs of ledges, a lift and wall attachments on the big dividing wall.
- **Satellite A, Low Isle (35 x 35 m):** low side ~-2 m, high side +4 m (a mound of structures). West of main.
- **Satellite B, High Isle (35 x 35 m):** sits higher (+10 m), south-east of main. Its top is a tower you can ledge-climb.

## Zones on the main platform
1. **Tunnel Block (NW ground layer):** a 40 x 30 m cluster of joined structures. Running tunnels (4 m tall, 3 m wide) loop through it, plus one crouch tunnel (2.4 m) and one crawl tunnel (1.7 m). Roofs at ~5 m make a rooftop layer, reached by ledges, a ramp and wall attachments. A fan lifts you up a shaft.
2. **Central Plaza:** deliberately open (~30 x 30 m) with low cover (crates, half-walls) and a climbable centre tower (ledges every ~3 m).
3. **High Plateau (east, +6 m):** the dividing wall facing the plaza has ledges, two holes you can run through, a ramp and a lift. On top: an arcade tunnel along the plateau, two huts with a roof bridge, a watch tower.
4. **South-west:** open ground with a few small structures, bounce pad up to the rooftops.
5. **Boundaries:** north wall tall (12 m, hard to leave) but covered in ledges, windows and a high walkway along its top. West and south-east borders open onto the pit.

## Routes off the main platform
- **To A (west):** an easy wide walkway (3 m, no rails) and a harder stepping-stone line (gaps 4–7 m).
- **To B (south-east):** the **pit maze**: narrow walkable paths (2.5 m) with turns, dead-end-free, some gaps to hop, one moving platform; and a bounce-pad launch from the plateau edge for the brave.
- **A to B:** a long narrow walkway around the south (no rails; abilities knock people off).

## Props (light touch)
A few crates, railings on some edges only, 2–3 bounce pads, 1–2 fans, vents on structure walls, pillars under the walkway. Plain moving platforms (no strips).

## Spawns
12 SpawnPoints spread across the main platform zones and the two isles (team modes split along the long axis).
